## 5. Limitations

- **Index level only.** Nothing here applies to a futures or ETF implementation. Tradability is not tested.
- **Hypothetical cost.** 0.10% round-trip is an owner-selected research assumption, not a verified cost. It does not represent broker, exchange, statutory, futures or ETF costs.
- **Few independent observations.** Development contains about 14 years and validation about 7. Monthly observations within a regime are not independent. p-values and intervals are approximate.
- **Only 6 entries per period (12-month).** Results depend on a small number of trend changes. The validation result is dominated by a single year (2017 contributes more than the whole log return), and removing the 5 best long months turns it negative.
- **Survivorship and index construction.** The index series is the current NIFTY 50 index history. Index composition changes over time are not controlled.
- **Calendar.** BSE calendar is not used (SENSEX excluded). The NSE-equity calendar was applied to NIFTY, and 46 non-calendar bars were excluded.
- **Count correction** (section 0, item 1): development counts are 167 and 166, not 168 and 167 as previously documented.
- **Not checked:** the 2022+ holdout was not inspected, and no conclusion about any post-2021 period is drawn.

## 6. Conclusion (evidence-based)

1. **Primary hypothesis (12-month NIFTY index time-series momentum, close-to-close, theoretical index level): REJECTED.** It fails the pre-registered acceptance criteria in both development (criterion 5) and validation (criteria 2, 5, 6).
2. **Companion (open-to-open): REJECTED** in both periods, consistent with the primary.
3. **Lookback sensitivity (6 and 9 months): no lookback passes all criteria in both periods.** No alternative lookback is adopted.
4. **Development returns look strong on net return and drawdown, but the timing test does not support them.** The time-shift null p = 0.156 means the observed alignment is not distinguishable, at the 5% level, from random realignments of the same signal. The development result therefore does not show that timing added value.
5. **Validation shows no risk-adjusted advantage** over buy-and-hold, and its net result depends on one year.
6. **What this does not show:** it does not prove the signal has no value in any other index, period or implementation. It is a single pre-registered test with a hypothetical cost.
