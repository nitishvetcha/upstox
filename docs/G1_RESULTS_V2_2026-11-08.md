## Interpretation

- **Gross excess returns are small.** The largest is about 6 bps at the 15-minute 1-hour horizon (I-2). The 1-hour 3-hour I-2 result of about 12 bps rests on 935 validation occurrences concentrated in two stocks with 30 or more occurrences. Round-trip costs in the intraday protocol are roughly 20–30 bps, so no cell covers costs.
- **I-2 (breakout with volume) and I-5 (opening-range breakout) are the only consistent positives.** I-2 at 15 minutes, 1 hour is positive in validation (+6.4 bps, CI [2.4, 10.4]) and development (+3.0 bps, CI [0.3, 5.7]), and fails only at session end. I-5 at 15 minutes, 1 hour is positive in both periods (+2.2 bps each), with a lower bound above zero, and fails at session end. In validation, I-2 is positive in all four regimes; I-5 is positive in three of four and slightly negative in high-volatility downtrends.
- **Multiplicity.** Each grid has 8 signals × 3–4 horizons × 2 periods. Several cells cross zero by chance at this scale, and horizons overlap. The I-2 and I-5 results are a lead, not a finding.
- **I-3 (momentum pullback) is significantly negative** at 15 minutes: validation −5.3 bps at session end (CI [−9.0, −1.6]), development −4.8 bps (CI [−8.0, −1.5]), and negative in every validation regime. The registered signal is long-only, so this says long pullbacks underperform the matched baseline. It does not establish a profitable short without a separately registered mirror hypothesis, which has not been run.
- **I-1 (trend continuation)** is significantly negative at the 15-minute one-bar horizon in validation (−0.5 bps) and not significant elsewhere.
- **I-4, I-6, I-7 and I-8** show no consistent excess. I-7 has too few occurrences (under 2,500 per period) to conclude anything.
- **Stock dispersion is large.** Per-stock excess for I-2 and I-5 ranges from about −14 to +46 bps, on roughly 60–100 occurrences per stock. Per-stock results are noisy and are not stock recommendations.

## Limits

Survivorship bias (today's 50 constituents). Long direction only. Unadjusted vendor prices, with the 2003–2004 BAJFINANCE and 2025 jumps excluded by rule. Gross of costs. Overlapping observations; the date-clustered interval is an approximation. Results describe 2022 to mid-2025 only.
